# S119 - canvas: compact responsive toolbar
[Overview](../BASED.md)

Reduce the floating toolbar footprint and keep every control visible in short
canvas viewports.

## Context

The floating canvas toolbar uses large controls in one vertical column. On a
short viewport the lower controls can fall beyond the visible canvas.

Relevant surfaces:

- [`styles.css`](../../packages/canvas/src/components/FloatingCanvasToolbar/styles.css)
- [`index.tsx`](../../packages/canvas/src/components/FloatingCanvasToolbar/index.tsx)
- [`ToolButton.tsx`](../../packages/canvas/src/components/FloatingCanvasToolbar/ToolButton.tsx)

## Plan

- Reduce control, icon, label, and outer spacing.
- At short viewport heights, arrange the tool list in two columns so it uses
  fewer vertical rows.
- Keep collapse and sidebar controls full-width across the responsive panel.

## Acceptance criteria

- The default floating toolbar is visibly smaller without losing controls.
- The toolbar stays in one column whenever its full height fits in the viewport.
- At viewport heights of 30rem or less, tool controls use two columns and stay
  visible.
- The toolbar remains usable, legible, and clear of the canvas sidebar.

- A mockup is not useful because the responsive behavior is best verified
