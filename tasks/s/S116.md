# S116 - canvas: delete only the retired persistence/projection architecture
[Overview](../BASED.md)

Delete Automerge and the parallel element/group projection architecture after
their responsibilities are replaced. Preserve the product.

## Deletion allowlist

- Automerge service, repository, document handles, routes, dependencies, and
  patch hook
- v1 canvas document schema and `TElement`/`TGroup` persistence vocabulary
- CRDT builders, change summaries, op inversion, projection signatures/hashes
- Projection registry/index/projectors/coordinator and derived-ID machinery
- Collaboration database tables and obsolete compatibility/migration fixtures

## Explicit keep/port list

- Canvas shell, visual design, grid, toolbar, style menu, context menu, help,
  accessibility, camera, gestures, drawing, transform, selection, and images
- Widget frames/actions, placement, clone policy, portals, runtime hosting,
  inspector integration, and draft Preview
- AI chat canvas extension, settings, widget-create integration, and sidebar
- Product behavior tests and screen-atlas acceptance coverage

Files in the keep/port list may be refactored or rewritten to remove legacy
dependencies, but their product capability may not be deleted without an
explicit separate product decision.
