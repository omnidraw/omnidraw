[Overview](../../BASED.md)

Collect fun widget/actor ideas for future SDK demos and fixtures.

## Context
Fun examples should showcase the widget UI + actor backend loop without feeling like boring CRUD.

## Plan
Keep a short idea bank. Pick one later when testing SDK ergonomics.

## NOTES
Fun widget/actor ideas:

1. Tiny Tamagotchi - feed, play, clean, sleep; actor tracks mood, hunger, chaos level.
2. Dungeon Door - knock/open/pick lock; actor handles loot, traps, and door mood.
3. Potion Brewer - combine ingredients; actor calculates healing, poison, explosion, glitter.
4. Goblin Negotiator - dialogue choices; actor tracks trust, greed, and insults remembered.
5. Magic 8-Ball Oracle - ask questions; actor returns dramatic prophecy history.
6. Office Plant Simulator - water, rotate, compliment; actor tracks happiness and wilting.
7. Mood Weather - emotional weather UI; actor changes sunny/foggy/thunderstorm state.
8. Cyberpunk Vending Machine - buy weird snacks; actor manages stock, credits, attitude.
9. Haunted Mirror - type thoughts; actor replies with spooky distorted reflections.
10. Tiny Space Probe - scan/boost/land; actor tracks fuel, discoveries, damage.
11. Alien Translator - translate phrases; actor stores diplomatic incidents.
12. Dice Goblin Casino - roll/gamble; actor tracks luck, debt, goblin commentary.
13. Pet Rock With Opinions - actor tracks boredom and gives judgmental responses.
14. Dream Generator - add ingredients; actor creates surreal dream logs.
15. Wizard Spellbook - combine spell words; actor determines spell outcomes.
16. Tiny Civilization - farm/build/research/party; actor tracks population, food, disasters.
17. Quest Giver - offers quests; actor tracks accepted quests, rewards, reputation.
18. Monster Mood Ring - actor receives events and changes monster expression/personality.
19. Pocket Black Hole - feed objects; actor tracks mass, danger level, weird emissions.
20. Interdimensional Radio - tune frequency; actor returns strange broadcasts.
