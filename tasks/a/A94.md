# A94 - canvas recovery: restore product parity on the new authorities
[Overview](../BASED.md)

Recover the canvas, widget, and AI product experience deleted during the first
A92/S116 cutover while keeping CanvasService and WidgetStateService as the sole
authorities.

## Safety and references

- Recovery branch: `codex/canvas-authority-repair`
- Authority-cutover checkpoint: `3db71817`
- Pre-cutover product reference: `396e9b45`
- Visual reference: `docs/internal/screens/SCREENS.md`

Do not restore directories wholesale. Classify each candidate as:

1. presentation/product policy to restore and port;
2. legacy authority code to leave deleted; or
3. mixed code to split at a new adapter boundary.

## Plan

### Phase 1 - inventory and boundaries

- Build a retained-feature/file/test matrix from the pre-cutover source.
- Define direct Cangine/editor and CanvasDocumentService ports needed by the
  restored product.
- Identify WidgetStateService ports for widget lifecycle and Preview.

### Phase 2 - canvas product shell

- Restore the established canvas layout, grid, toolbar, menus, styling, camera,
  keyboard behavior, selection, transforms, and drawing tools.
- Replace old CRDT/projector calls with direct Cangine/editor operations and
  CanvasDocumentService commands.

### Phase 3 - widget and AI integrations

- Restore widget frames/actions, placement, portals, and runtime mounting.
- Complete direct Draft placement and live draft Preview through
  [`A96`](./A96.md) instead of restoring the pre-cutover repeated-build loop.
- Restore AI chat canvas mounting, settings, widget-create result handling, and
  sidebar integrations.
- Route durable canvas writes through CanvasService and widget JSON state through
  WidgetStateService.

### Phase 4 - qualification

- Keep focused product tests on the current authority adapters.
- Verify atlas canvas states `10` through `17` at a consistent desktop viewport.
- Run focused authority, widget-host, build, and release gates.
