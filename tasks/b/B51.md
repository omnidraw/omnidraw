# B51 - widget reload: moved manifest conflicts with persisted actor slug
[Overview](../BASED.md)

Reloading a moved or republished widget updates its persisted actor definition instead of attempting a duplicate insert.

## Context

Actor definition synchronization identified persisted definitions only by manifest path, while the database enforces unique names and slugs. Moving a manifest could therefore attempt an insert and fail with `UNIQUE constraint failed: actor_definitions.slug`.

Relevant files:

- `packages/service-actor/src/core/tx.actor-definitions.ts`
- `packages/service-db/src/DbServiceTurso/tx.actor.ts`
- `packages/service-actor/tests/tx.actor-definitions.test.ts`

## Plan

1. Match persisted definitions by manifest path, slug, or name.
2. Let definition updates target the previous slug and persist a changed slug.
3. Cover moved-manifest synchronization and DB identity updates.
